## Executive Overview

To build elder safety technology that survives real-world deployment, Tether conducted field and survey research across the three distinct stakeholders in the care continuum:
1. **The Wearer (The Senior Citizen / End-User):** Needs safety without humiliation, stigma, or loss of autonomy.
2. **The Buyer (The Adult Child / Family Guardian):** Needs relief from chronic vigilance anxiety without damaging the parent-child bond.
3. **The Institutional Operator (Nurses & Facility Administrators):** Needs reduced labor burdens and high-precision triage without alert fatigue.

Across all three cohorts, traditional market solutions (panic pendants, invasive cameras, consumer smartwatches) fail for the exact same underlying reasons: **they treat seniors like hospital patients, turn adult children into nagging wardens, and overwhelm care staff with false alarms.**

## 1. The Wearer (The Senior): The Stigma & Secret Scare Trap

### The Lived Reality
* **The "Secret Scare" Phenomenon:** Field interviews reveal that seniors living alone frequently experience dizziness, orthostatic hypotension, or minor slips in high-risk zones (such as bathrooms or getting out of bed at night). Crucially, **they deliberately keep these incidents secret from their adult children**. They fear that reporting a scare will trigger panic, emotional distress, or a loss of independence (e.g., being forced to relocate, move into assisted living, or accept a full-time attendant).
* **The Device Stigma Barrier:** Seniors overwhelmingly reject traditional emergency pendants, lanyards, and bulky "panic buttons." Wearing an obvious plastic beeper feels infantilizing and acts as a visible public badge of frailty. When forced to adopt them, seniors leave them on nightstands, in drawers, or hang them on door hooks where they are useless during a real crisis.
* **The Usability & Charging Burden:** Existing smartwatches require daily charging, complex touchscreen navigation, and ongoing Bluetooth pairing. For an aging senior with reduced dexterity or mild cognitive decline, daily charging routines fail rapidly, leading to permanent device abandonment.

### How Tether Actively Solves It
* **Tenet I — Senior Dignity & Zero-Stigma Hardware:** Tether is engineered as a **discreet, screenless fabric band** resembling high-end textile apparel rather than a medical appliance. There is no screen to cause anxiety or confusion, and no public stigma. Neighbors and peers simply see a stylish wristband.
* **Autonomous, Passive Background Protection:** The wearer is never required to remember to press a button during acute trauma, syncope, or a debilitating fall. Tether’s passive biometrics and dual-vector fall detection run quietly in the background without patronizing the senior.
* **Weekly-Plus Battery Life & True 24/7 Wearability:** By removing power-draining color displays and utilizing ultra-low-power edge processing, Tether eliminates the daily charging chore. It is water-resistant and breathable, remaining on the senior's wrist where falls actually happen: in the shower and during nighttime bathroom visits.

## 2. The Buyer (The Adult Child): The "Warden vs. Patient" Paradox

### The Lived Reality
* **Chronic Vigilance Burnout:** Adult children who live in another neighborhood, city, or country carry constant background anxiety. An unanswered phone call or unread message immediately triggers catastrophic dread (*"Did they collapse? Are they lying helpless on the floor?"*).
* **The "Warden vs. Patient" Trap:** Because there is no passive safety net, the adult child is forced into constant, intrusive check-ins (*"Did you take your pills? Did you lock the front door? Why weren't you picking up at 2 PM?"*). This dynamic strips the senior of self-respect, creates mutual resentment, and reduces loving parent-child relationships into administrative interrogations.
* **Parent Refusal as the #1 Commercial Barrier:** Adult children represent the paying customer with high willingness-to-pay ($30–$50/month subscription). However, their single greatest hesitation before purchasing is: ***"My parent will refuse to wear it, and it will end up sitting in a drawer."***

### How Tether Actively Solves It
* **Tenet II — Caregiver Autonomy & Relationship Restoration:** Tether liberates the adult child from vigilance anxiety. With continuous passive monitoring, no news is genuinely good news. When the child calls, it is to catch up as a loving son or daughter, not to audit daily survival. Both generations regain personal freedom and peace of mind.
* **Wearer Compliance Drives Real Commercial ROI:** Because Tether solves the dignity and aesthetic problem for the senior, wearer compliance remains near 100%. The adult child can confidently purchase a subscription knowing the hardware will actually be worn.
* **Tenet III — Emergency NFC Passport & Community Safety Net:** In an emergency, adult children are often hours away and unable to recall complex medication schedules under stress. Tether’s integrated NFC passport enables any first responder, hospital physician, or good Samaritan to tap the band with a smartphone and instantly access vital allergies, chronic conditions, and emergency contacts—turning the surrounding community into a respectful safety net.

## 3. The Institutional Operator (Nurses & Retirement Facilities): Workflow Efficiency & Liability

### The Lived Reality
* **Crushing Routine Workload:** In assisted living and senior care facilities, staffing shortages mean 1 nurse or care assistant is often responsible for 15 to 30+ residents. Routine manual vitals rounds (blood pressure, temperature, pulse) consume 1 to 2 hours per shift, pulling skilled staff away from direct, high-acuity patient care.
* **The Delayed Discovery Window:** Unobserved falls during night shifts remain an acute liability. A resident who falls and remains undiscovered on the floor for 30+ minutes faces severe complications (hypothermia, rhabdomyolysis, pressure sores, prolonged hospitalization), leading to immense family distress and costly regulatory penalties.
* **Alert Fatigue & Distrust of Legacy Tech:** Facilities frequently experience bed sensors, chair pads, and motion detectors triggering high rates of false alarms. Over time, care teams suffer from alarm fatigue and become desensitized, slowing response times across the board.

### How Tether Actively Solves It
* **Automated Telemetry Rounds:** Tether passively logs vital trends 24/7. This cuts manual vitals rounds in half, liberating nursing hours while providing continuous physiological baselines that flag early deterioration hours before an acute collapse.
* **Immediate Fall Triage & Liability Reduction:** Autonomous impact and disorientation detection alerts the central nursing station immediately when an incident occurs. This closes the hazardous delayed discovery window, drastically improving recovery outcomes and safeguarding institutional compliance.
* **Centralized Multi-Resident Dashboard:** Instead of noisy, localized buzzers, ward managers receive high-fidelity, prioritized alerts routed to centralized station monitors or mobile staff devices, minimizing false positives and eliminating alert fatigue.

2. **Dual-Vector Go-To-Market:**
